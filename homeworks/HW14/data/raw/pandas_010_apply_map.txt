TITLE: Apply, map, and vectorization
SOURCE: synthetic://pandas/apply_map_vectorization

Pandas users often choose between vectorized expressions, map, apply, and row-wise logic.
Vectorized operations are generally preferred because they are faster and more idiomatic.
The map method is commonly used on Series for dictionary-like replacement or simple element-wise transformations.

The apply method can be useful, but it is sometimes overused.
Applying Python functions row by row may be slower than expressing the same transformation with built-in vectorized operations.
In performance-sensitive code, users should first look for native pandas or NumPy alternatives.
