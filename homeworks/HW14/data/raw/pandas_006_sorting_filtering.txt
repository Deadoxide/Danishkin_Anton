TITLE: Sorting and filtering
SOURCE: synthetic://pandas/sorting_filtering

Filtering in pandas usually relies on boolean conditions.
Users may compare numeric columns, test membership with isin, or combine conditions with logical operators.
Parentheses are important when combining multiple boolean expressions.

Sorting is often done with sort_values for rows and sort_index for labels.
A DataFrame can be sorted by one column or multiple columns with different ascending flags.
Filtering and sorting are among the most frequent operations in exploratory analysis and data cleaning.

For readability, many analysts build intermediate masks and then apply them to the DataFrame.
