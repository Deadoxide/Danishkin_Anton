TITLE: Datetime handling
SOURCE: synthetic://pandas/datetime

Datetime data in pandas is commonly converted with to_datetime.
Once a column has datetime dtype, users can access components such as year, month, day, and weekday with the dt accessor.
Time-based indexing enables convenient slicing, resampling, and rolling analysis.

Datetime handling is important in logs, finance, monitoring, and forecasting tasks.
Users should pay attention to parsing formats, time zones, and invalid values.
After conversion, sorting by time and setting a DatetimeIndex often makes downstream analysis easier.
