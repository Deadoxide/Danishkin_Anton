TITLE: Pivot tables
SOURCE: synthetic://pandas/pivot_table

Pivot tables in pandas summarize data across dimensions and are useful for reporting and analysis.
The pivot_table function supports aggregation, grouping by multiple dimensions, and filling missing combinations.
Users can choose index, columns, values, and aggregation functions.

Pivot tables are especially convenient when analysts want spreadsheet-like summaries directly in Python.
They can often replace more verbose groupby plus reshape workflows.
